A Few Things to Keep in Mind

  • Be respectful and kind to everyone.
  • Be cautious about who you trust online.
  • Do not share private or sensitive personal information.
  • Do not send money or purchase anything from someone messaging you through The Written Bond.
  • Be cautious about meeting anyone in person. If something feels suspicious, do not meet them.
  • If someone is bothering you or making you uncomfortable, block them so you no longer see their messages.
